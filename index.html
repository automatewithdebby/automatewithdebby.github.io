Approach

How I work

01

Systems, not workflows

A workflow moves data between two apps. A system covers the whole path: first trigger to final output, including what happens when something fails, when a human needs to step in, and when the volume doubles.

02

The right tool, not my favourite one

Vapi, ElevenLabs, Voiceflow, n8n, Zapier. Voice AI and chat are not the same problem, and neither is a nightly batch job. I pick per problem, and I tell you why, including when the answer is that you don't need automation at all.

03

No black boxes

Every build comes with a walkthrough of how it works and where to change it. If you can't run it without me, I haven't finished the job.
